    A QUICK GUIDE

    Make yourself comfortable.

    1. Place the phone flat between you. The far side is rotated for your opponent.
    2. Shield your cards with your hand. Hold either private card to see them; let go to hide. Only one person can peek at a time.
    3. On your turn, tap colored chips to build a wager. Selected chips stay pending until you confirm. Undo returns the last selection. Use Check / Call for the exact amount owed.
    4. The dealer posts the small blind and acts first before the flop. The other player acts first on later streets.
    5. At showdown, both hands are public. After a fold, cards stay hidden. Tap Next hand when both players are ready.

    Play-money chips only. There is no timer, rake, or network opponent. Uncalled chips are returned. Ties split the pot; any odd chip goes to the non-dealer.

    Your privacy

    This is a game between trusted friends. Shielding cards prevents casual glances, not screenshots or technical inspection. The full game is saved only in this browser when storage is available. No game data is sent to a server.

    Keep it handy

    In Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen. After a successful online load, the game can work offline once its assets are cached. Browser storage can be cleared; it is not a permanent backup.

    Betting and dealing follow Poker TDA rules. House choices: fixed blinds, no timers, both live hands shown at showdown, no muck option, and an explicit next-hand button.
